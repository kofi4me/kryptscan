About KryptScan

Practical security assessment for teams responsible for real systems.

KryptScan is developed by KryptNet LLC for IT professionals, security teams, consultants, and MSPs that need repeatable vulnerability assessment and authorized ethical testing.

Our purpose

Technology environments change continuously. KryptScan helps authorized defenders identify exposed services, security weaknesses, and remediation priorities without replacing professional judgment.

Responsible testing

Every assessment requires confirmation of ownership or written authorization. Deceptive, destructive, disruptive, or unauthorized use is prohibited.

Reports built for action

Paid assessments provide human-readable PDF reporting and structured JSON for analytics, automation, and approved integrations.

Support

Help with accounts, assessments, and reports.

Before contacting support

Include your registered email, scan ID, the approximate time of the issue, and a description of what happened. Never send passwords, tokens, private keys, or customer credentials.

{% if policy == 'terms' %}

Terms of Service

Use of KryptScan is limited to lawful, authorized security assessment. Users are responsible for target authorization, scope accuracy, and compliance with applicable laws and contracts.

{% elif policy == 'privacy' %}

Privacy Policy

KryptScan processes account, authorization, scan, report, and security-event data to provide and protect the service. Sensitive report access is restricted to the owning account and authorized administrators.

{% elif policy == 'acceptable-use' %}

Acceptable Use Policy

Only test systems you own or have written permission to assess. Malware deployment, persistence, denial of service, credential theft, data exfiltration, evasion, and unauthorized access are prohibited.

{% elif policy == 'credits-refunds' %}

Credits and Refund Policy

Credits are reserved when a scan starts and consumed when the assessment completes. Credits are automatically returned for qualifying KryptScan platform failures. Target unavailability or user-supplied scope errors may still consume credits after scanning resources have been used.

{% else %}

Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

Report suspected KryptScan vulnerabilities privately to KryptNet LLC. Do not access customer data, disrupt service, publish unverified findings, or exceed the minimum testing needed to demonstrate an issue.
